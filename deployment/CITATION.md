# How to cite PCD-SPECTRA

## Required research citation

If you use PCD-SPECTRA in research, cite the associated manuscript as:

> Zhan, L., Ding, S., Dong, F., Chen, G.-H., & Li, K. *Quantifying Photon Counting Detector (PCD) Performance Using PCD-CT Images. Part II. Detector Spectral Response Function of Clinical Scanners.* *Medical Physics* (2026). Accepted for publication.


## Software authors

Authors: Shanli Ding, Linying Zhan, Ke Li

The software author list is separate from the publication author list above.
`CITATION.cff` records the software authors in its top-level `authors` field
and the publication authors in `preferred-citation.authors`.

## Suggested software acknowledgement

> This work used PCD-SPECTRA (Photon-Counting Detector Spectral Response
> Analysis), version 1.0.0.

Final DOI, volume, and page or article number will be added when assigned by
the publisher. The accepted-paper citation above is usable in the meantime.
