# How to cite PCD-SPECTRA

## Required research citation

If you use PCD-SPECTRA in research, cite the associated manuscript as:

> Zhan, L., Ding, S., Dong, F., Chen, G.-H., & Li, K. *Quantifying Photon
> Counting Detector (PCD) Performance Using PCD-CT Images. Part II. Detector
> Spectral Response Function of Clinical Scanners.* Manuscript under review at
> *Medical Physics*.


## Suggested software acknowledgement

> This work used PCD-SPECTRA (Photon-Counting Detector Spectral Response
> Analysis), version 1.0.0.

## Wording for an anonymous peer-review document

When a journal requires author and manuscript details to remain blinded, use:

> [Authors and manuscript details redacted for peer review.]

Do not use the redacted wording in public research outputs; use the full
provisional citation above until final publication details are available.
